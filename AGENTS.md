# Agent guide

**English** · [Slovenščina](AGENTS.sl.md)

Printable A4 routine sheets for young children (morning, day, evening). They hang on a fridge; the child follows the pictures and puts a magnet on each finished step's circle, adults read the order and the planned time. Public repository: no names or photos of real people.

## Files

| Path                              | Content                                                                  |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `routines/<lang>/<name>.json`     | A routine: sheets, each with title, sky, steps (label, image id, color). |
| `images/<id>.jpg` + `.json`       | 1024 × 1024 image and metadata (model, prompt, keywords, edits, check).  |
| `images/README.md`                | Gallery of all images, generated by `tools/build.py`.                    |
| `output/<lang>-<name>.html`       | Generated sheets for printing. Never edit by hand.                       |
| `web/`                            | Sheet maker in the browser (`index.html`, `app.js`); keywords match.     |
| `tools/build.py`                  | Generator: routines → output, and `web/data.js` for the sheet maker.     |
| `tools/check_images.py`           | Validation run in CI.                                                    |
| `tools/copyright_check.py`        | Copyright gate (Vision Web Detection).                                   |
| `tools/lens_check.py`             | Manual Google Lens check through a debugging Chrome.                     |
| `docs/PROMPTS.md`                 | Image style text, prompts and lessons.                                   |
| `.agents/skills/copyright-check/` | How to check images before adding or merging them.                       |
| `docs/copyright-investigations/`  | One write-up per image match that needed a closer look.                  |

## Commands

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python3 tools/check_images.py   # must print 0 errors
python3 tools/build.py          # rebuild output/ after any change to routines or images
python3 tools/preview.py        # then refresh docs/preview.jpg (README); needs Chrome and pdftoppm
```

Check a change visually: print the output file to PDF with headless Chrome and look at it (`google-chrome --headless=new --print-to-pdf=out.pdf file://$PWD/output/<file>.html`).

Test touch in the sheet maker (CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`) in a headless Chrome and with the target scrolled into view: a Chrome window in the background draws no frames, so the call never returns. Check horizontal overflow against the emulated device width (390, 320), not `innerWidth`: with mobile emulation the viewport grows with the content.

## Layout and its limits

- A step is one object: square image (58 mm; 53 mm at 8 steps, larger at 5 steps or fewer: 67, 81, 98.5 mm at 5, 4, 3 or fewer; rule `layout()` in `tools/build.py`) and a frame (84 × 18 mm) with the label and a time field (`__ : __`), plus a 35 mm circle for the magnet next to the image, so the child links picture and magnet. All sizes are CSS variables from the paper size (`--page-w`, `--page-h`, `--img`, `--label` in `tools/build.py`); A4 now, US Letter later by changing the page variables. Image, frame and circle share one outline and background; the frame background is the color of the image strip that fades into the frame (inner 12 %, at the frame's height, measured separately for the left and right edge, without the darkest quarter of pixels, lifted to a pastel if dark), measured by the generator.
- Images alternate left and right; the first image sits 1.5 mm under the title row and the last 1.5 mm above the footer, the centres evenly spaced between (no empty band). The size is the larger of two modes, rounded down to 0.5 mm, 3 mm clearance between objects. Narrow frames (6 to 8 steps): images on the same side keep the clearance, image + frame + image = 200 mm. Wide frames (`.rt-wide`, 5 steps or fewer): the frame runs from its image to the far page edge, over the opposite column, so the next image must clear it (distance between centres at least image/2 + 9 mm + clearance), and an image wider than 81 mm also has to clear the neighbour's circle; at most 98.5 mm so the two columns keep the clearance. Number badges (10 mm) are concentric with the image corner (radius 7 mm = badge radius + 1 mm gap + border), the nested-radius rule. In narrow mode width is the binding limit: image + frame + image = 200 mm (A4 minus 5 mm side margins); every mm of image takes 2 mm from the frame and its label, because a frame reaches into the height of the opposite image.
- A 14 mm footer above the bottom margin carries the licence (CC0), the address and a QR code (`web/qr.svg`, made by `tools/qr.py`; regenerate only when the address changes).
- Margins 5 mm top and sides, 14 mm bottom: many home printers cannot print the bottom edge. Older and pro Epson models need 14 mm for single sheets (Epson SureColor reference guide), HP DeskJet about 12.7 mm (HP Community, DeskJet 2700); newer Epsons manage 3 mm. Keep the footer (QR code) above 14 mm.
- `web/app.js` `sheetHtml()` mirrors `tools/build.py` `sheet_html()`: change both together. `web/data.js` is generated; never edit it.
- Labels do not wrap. 13 pt bold uppercase fits about 17 characters (`POSPRAVI POSTELJO`, 44.2 of 45.0 mm). After changing labels, measure in headless Chrome that each label's `scrollWidth` fits the frame width minus the magnet circle, paddings and gap (its parent `.rt-text` grows with the label, so it is no measure; `web/app.js` `labelRoom()` does it right).
- Label color is darkened automatically to at least 4.5 : 1 contrast against the frame (WCAG 2.2, 1.4.3).
- 6 to 7 steps per sheet (at most 8). Split long routines into more sheets rather than shrinking images.

## Images

- Follow `docs/PROMPTS.md`: one watercolor style, real rooms, no clouds (except night), no text, brands, flags or known characters. Generate; never compose an image from pasted parts with code.
- Image models tend to produce pale images. Raise contrast once with Pillow: `ImageOps.autocontrast(cutoff=(0.5, 0.3))`, `ImageEnhance.Contrast(1.1)`, `ImageEnhance.Color(1.12)`, `UnsharpMask(radius=2, percent=50, threshold=3)`. Crop in the file, not with CSS, so the edge color is measured on the visible edge.
- Copy the prompt and the reference images into the image's `.json` exactly as passed to the model (from the tool call), never rewritten from `docs/PROMPTS.md`. What is not known stays `unknown`.
- Before adding any image, follow the skill `.agents/skills/copyright-check/` on the raw and on the final file, and record the result in its `.json`.

## Rules

- This repository is self-contained: everything an agent needs (rules, skills, tools) is in it, and nothing refers to files outside it. `CLAUDE.md` and `GEMINI.md` only import this file.
- Never weaken the copyright gate (`.github/workflows/copyright-gate.yml`): it must stay on `pull_request_target`, must not check out or run pull request code, and must fail closed.
- When a parent makes a routine for their own child, keep the child's name and other personal details out of commits and pull requests.
- The examples (`routines/sl/primer.json`, `routines/en/example.json`; the sheet maker loads them) model good habits, e.g. screens at least an hour before sleep (KidsHealth), hands washed before meals, teeth after the last meal; the evening includes medicine and care, to support families who need it. The Slovenian morning has no breakfast, because Slovenian kindergartens serve it; the English one keeps it.
- Keep `README`, `CONTRIBUTING` and `AGENTS` in English and Slovenian in sync (`*.sl.md`). `README` is for parents, without commands; building, agents and contributing live in `CONTRIBUTING`.
- An expert discussion of the routines or sheets invites at least a Montessori educator, a child development expert and a specialist in children's product safety and labelling (e.g. toy warning symbols), besides a parent and an investor; a discussion of texts for parents also invites a sales copywriter.
- Texts for parents state an effect without a read source as a possibility ("can"), never as a fact. A point the maintainer added from practice (e.g. a familiar order can calm a child, from children's mindfulness books) stays, phrased that way; reviews soften such points, they do not remove them.
- Colours are child-friendly and gender-neutral: playroom yellow, orange, red, green, teal and blue, no pink or purple, on the sheets and in the sheet maker. Step colours keep a white step number at 3 : 1 or more.
- Magnets: recommend 35 mm or more, set in plastic; the US small-parts cylinder is 31.7 mm wide (CPSC 16 CFR 1501). Several swallowed magnets attract each other through the intestines.
