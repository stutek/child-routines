# Child routines

**English** · [Slovenščina](README.sl.md)

**[Make your sheet →](https://stutek.github.io/child-routines/)**

A4 sheets with a daily routine for children aged 3 and up: morning, day and evening. Your child follows the pictures. They mark each finished step with a magnet on its circle or a tick. Adults read the order and the time.

![Three example sheets: morning, day and evening](docs/preview.jpg)

Free, no sign-up and no ads. What you type stays in your browser.

## Quick start

1. Open the [sheet maker](https://stutek.github.io/child-routines/). It is easiest on a computer. It opens with an example evening routine.
2. Pick the routine type and type the steps: we recommend 3 to 8, 3–4 to start with. Click a picture to change it. Type your own text to change the title and the steps; empty rows do not show on the sheet. Type a time, or turn off the *Time on the sheet* switch. Drag a row by its dots to change the order.
3. Click **Print**: A4, margins *None*, scale 100%. For a PDF file you can share, choose *Save as PDF* in the print window.
4. When you change or adjust the routine, print a new sheet.
5. To share the current view of the app with family and friends, use the *Share link* button.

## How to use it

- **Make the sheet together with your child.** You type the steps, your child picks the pictures.
- **Do the steps together for the first few days.**
- **Start with 3–4 steps.** When your child knows the routine, add more.
- **A magnet means “done”.** Don't promise gifts for magnets: a reward can take away the joy of the task.
- **The times on the sheet are for adults,** not for hurrying. If a step is skipped or the order gets mixed up, stay calm and try again next time.
- **When your child sleeps over at grandparents', an aunt's or an uncle's,** send the sheet along. A familiar order can give a child a sense of security.

Example sheets, ready to print, are [here](https://stutek.github.io/child-routines/output/en-example.html).

## Safety

- **Age:** the sheet is for children aged 3 and up.
- **Magnets:** use only magnets that are safe for children and chemically harmless. If a child swallows a magnet, see a doctor at once.
- **Pens:** for ticks, use only pens that are safe for children and chemically harmless.
- **Medicine:** an adult gives and keeps it. It is not a sweet.
- **Bath:** never leave a child alone in the bath.
- **Teeth:** follow your dentist's advice.

## Privacy

- The page uses no cookies, ads or visitor counters.
- What you type stays in your browser and is not sent to a server.
- A share link carries the sheet after the `#` sign. The browser does not send that part to the server ([RFC 3986, 3.5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3986#section-3.5)). Anyone with the link can see the sheet.
- The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs the visitor's IP address for security ([GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection), [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)).

## For advanced users

### Print without a connection

1. On the [project page](https://github.com/stutek/child-routines) click **Code**, then **Download ZIP**, and unpack it. The sheets need the `images/` folder next to `output/`, so opening a single file on GitHub does not work.
2. In Chrome, open `web/index.html` for your own sheet or `output/en-example.html` for the example.
3. Print as above.
