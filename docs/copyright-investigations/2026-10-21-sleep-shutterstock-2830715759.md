## Prompts

Both prompts read like language-model output and share its stock phrases ("children's … gouache", "crescent moon", "soft clouds", "no text, no letters"). The maintainer did not know the Shutterstock prompt or image when the sleep prompt was written.

Our prompt fixes the layout: "its head rests on a white pillow at the LEFT end of the bed, its body is stretched out under a patchwork quilt toward the RIGHT end". The Shutterstock prompt does not mention the layout, so its model chose the same one unprompted: it is the default way image models draw "a bear asleep in a bed". Every detail our prompt asks for and theirs does not (nightcap, heart on the footboard) is in our image and not in theirs.

## Assessment

1. **The shared part is an idea.** Slovenian Copyright and Related Rights Act (ZASP), Article 9(1): "Avtorskopravno niso varovane: 1. ideje, načela, odkritja" (ideas, principles and discoveries are not protected). A teddy bear asleep in a wooden bed under a patchwork quilt, with stars, moon and clouds, is an idea built from stock bedtime elements.
2. **The layout is common and was prescribed by our own prompt** (see above).
3. **No expression is shared.** Character design, drawing, quilt pattern, furniture, objects and technique all differ; no distinctive detail of the Shutterstock image appears in ours.
4. **The source is probably not protected by copyright at all.** ZASP, Article 10: "Avtor je fizična oseba, ki je ustvarila avtorsko delo" (the author is a natural person). In the United States, *Thaler v. Perlmutter* (D.C. Cir., March 2025) held that copyright requires a human author. The Shutterstock license is a contract with its buyers; this project never licensed or downloaded the asset.

**Risk:** low. Under the [copyright check](../../.agents/skills/copyright-check/SKILL.md) this is a visually similar result (same subject, different picture): accept.
